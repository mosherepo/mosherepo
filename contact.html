Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our AI and data development services

What is your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary based on complexity, but typically range from 2-6 months for AI solutions. Simple data analytics projects can be completed in 4-8 weeks, while complex machine learning systems may take 6-12 months. I provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation.

Do you work with startups or only enterprise clients?

I work with businesses of all sizes, from startups to Fortune 500 companies. I offer flexible engagement models and can adapt my services to fit different budgets and requirements. Many startups have successfully launched with my AI solutions.

What technologies do you specialize in?

I specialize in Python, R, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, and cloud platforms like AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure. I also work with data technologies like Apache Spark, Hadoop, and various databases. I stay current with the latest AI frameworks and tools.

Do you provide ongoing support after project completion?

Yes, I offer comprehensive support packages including monitoring, maintenance, updates, and optimization. I also provide training for your team to ensure smooth operation and can help with scaling as your business grows.

How do you ensure data security and privacy?

Data security is a top priority. I implement industry-standard encryption, secure data handling practices, and comply with regulations like GDPR and HIPAA. All projects include comprehensive security measures and regular security audits.

What is your pricing model?

I offer flexible pricing models including fixed-price projects, hourly consulting, and retainer agreements. Pricing depends on project complexity, timeline, and requirements. I provide detailed proposals with transparent pricing after our initial consultation.
