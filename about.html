My Journey in Data & AI

Over the past 20+ years, I've dedicated my career to pushing the boundaries of what's possible with artificial intelligence and data analytics. My journey began in the early 2000s when AI was still in its infancy, and I've been at the forefront of its evolution ever since.

I started as a software engineer with a passion for mathematics and statistics. As machine learning began to emerge as a powerful tool, I recognized its potential to transform industries. This led me to specialize in AI development, where I've since built hundreds of intelligent systems that drive real business value.

Today, I work with companies ranging from startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, helping them harness the power of AI to solve complex problems, optimize operations, and create new opportunities for growth.
